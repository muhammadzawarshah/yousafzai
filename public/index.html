News & Events

Introduction

The Yousafzai tribe, one of the most prominent lineages of the Pashtun people, has played an instrumental role in shaping the history and culture of the region. Under the esteemed leadership of Malak Ahmad Khan Yousafzai, the tribe migrated during the late 15th and early 16th centuries to what is now Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, where they established powerful strongholds in Dir, Swat, Malakand, Mardan, and Swabi.
Although the majority of the tribe settled in these areas, numerous Yousafzai communities continue to flourish in Afghanistan, Iran, and India. Renowned for their esteemed traditions, leadership, and invaluable contributions to Pashtun heritage, the Yousafzais remain a respected and historically significant tribe.
